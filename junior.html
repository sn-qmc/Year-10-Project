Junior School Digital Technology

Years 1–3

Students develop foundational digital skills using iPads and interactive technologies. Learning focuses on coding, creativity, problem solving and logical thinking.

Year 4

Year 4 students transition from touchscreen devices to keyboards using iPads and Chromebooks. They begin structured coding activities and explore Lego Spike to develop computational thinking.

Years 5–6

The BYOD programme begins in Year 5. Students use laptops for research, presentations and digital assessments while learning about digital citizenship and online safety.
