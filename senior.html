Senior Digital Technology

Year 11 – NCEA Level 1

Students learn programming, website development, and digital media. Python is introduced alongside HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Year 12 – NCEA Level 2

Students apply iterative design processes to develop digital outcomes, create websites, write programs, and evaluate risks and solutions.

Year 13 – NCEA Level 3

Students complete a client‑based project, developing an interactive website using advanced programming techniques and project management tools.
