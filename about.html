What is Technology?

Technology is the use of knowledge, tools, and systems to solve problems and improve the way humans live and interact with the world. From early tools and fire to the Internet and artificial intelligence, technology has shaped society.

Technological developments such as the printing press, telephone, and digital communication have reduced barriers to communication and enabled global interaction.

What is Digital Technology?

Digital Technology focuses specifically on electronic tools, systems, and applications that create, store, process, and share information. This includes software, websites, computer programs, networks, and digital media.
