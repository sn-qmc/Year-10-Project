Middle School Digital Technology

Year 7

Students are introduced to Digital Technology at QMC, the 1:1 laptop programme, and design projects that incorporate digital media and computational thinking.

Year 8

Students explore electronics, circuit design, laser cutting and sustainable energy. Projects combine hardware and software solutions.

Year 9

Students learn digital design, AI concepts, and apply the design cycle to create a video game that raises awareness for a good cause.

Year 10

Students become creators of digital technology, learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, electronics, robotics, and programming in real‑world contexts.
