Digital Technology at Queen Margaret College

Digital Technology is a key part of the S.T.E.A.M disciplines at QMC, alongside Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Mathematics. Students develop digital fluency that supports learning across all subject areas.

Being fluent in digital technologies enables students to:

  • Solve complex problems
  • Think critically and creatively
  • Collaborate effectively
  • Engage with future career pathways

At every year level, students learn to be responsible digital citizens, understanding digital ethics, online safety, and the impact of technology on society.
